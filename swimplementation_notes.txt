- Decoy Attack II 
Stacks 1 debuff object up to 4 times, each one has an independant 10s timer. (max -2,-8)
Or: Dmg +2 or +8 if hits. 
Toggled on and off by "use decoy". 
Enemies with this feat always use it. 
Being damaged, even 0 dmg, removes this effect.
